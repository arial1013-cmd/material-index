🚫 Be 動詞否定句練習

Be Verb Negative Sentences Practice

📚 練習說明 Instructions

將下列肯定句改成否定句。記得在 be 動詞後面加上 not！

Transform the statements below into negative sentences. Remember to add "not" after the be verb!

🔑 重要規則 Key Rules:

  • be 動詞後面直接加 not（Add "not" right after the be verb）
  • 主詞位置不變（Subject stays in the same place）
  • 句尾維持句號（Keep the period at the end，不是問號）
  • 可以用完整式或縮寫式，兩種都算對（Full form or contraction — both are accepted）
is not → isn't
are not → aren't
am not → 沒有縮寫，只能說 I'm not

範例 Example:

Statement: He is a teacher.

Negative: He is not a teacher. / He isn't a teacher.
